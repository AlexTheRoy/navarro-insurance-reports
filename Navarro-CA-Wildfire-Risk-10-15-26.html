Scoring system

Public federal data, built the way commercial wildfire scores are weighted

What goes into the score
  • Wildfire riskNRI wildfire risk score: burn probability times the value exposed, scored 0 to 100 nationally.
  • ConcentrationShare of residents in tracts rated Relatively High or Very High for wildfire.
  • ResilienceNRI community resilience: local capacity to recover, a small adjustment.
  • AvailabilityCarrier wildfire scores (Verisk FireLine, Cotality, Zesty.ai) are property-level and not published by ZIP. This composite uses public federal data on the same ingredients.

Tier rules
  • ExtremeResident-weighted NRI wildfire score 95+, or 50%+ of residents in high-risk tracts
  • HighScore 80+, or 20%+ of residents in high-risk tracts
  • ModerateScore 45+, 5%+ of residents in high-risk tracts, or any single tract at 99+
  • LowEverything else

The bundle question

Auto on its own price, the renters bundle where it fits

How to use the tiers
  • LowLead with the Auto price and offer the renters bundle to every renter; 283 of your 442 leads who did not report owning a home live in Low-tier ZIPs.
  • ModerateSame approach. With homeowners, a friendly question about their current home carrier opens the conversation without a home quote.
  • HighHomeowners here may be dealing with changes to their home coverage; let the Auto policy win on its own price and coverage.
  • ExtremeQuote Auto on its own merits and point homeowners to available property options, such as the California FAIR Plan paired with a difference-in-conditions policy.
442 of 579 leads (76.3%) did not say they own their home: the renters bundle audience. Allstate filed with the California Department of Insurance on August 31, 2026 to resume writing new homeowners policies; the filing is under review and not yet open for new business.
